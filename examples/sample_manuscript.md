# Sample manuscript for testing the verifier

This file deliberately mixes good and bad citations so you can see every verdict type.

## Introduction

Deep learning allows computational models composed of multiple processing layers to learn representations of data with multiple levels of abstraction [1]. Deep learning has eliminated the need for feature engineering in every domain, cutting error rates by 90% [1]. The Transformer architecture relies entirely on attention mechanisms, dispensing with recurrence and convolutions [2].

Large language models now produce citations with near-perfect accuracy in systematic reviews [3]. Early reports linked the MMR vaccine to developmental disorders in children [4]. Residual connections enable the training of networks with more than 100 layers [5]. Reporting guidance for systematic reviews was updated in 2021 [6, 7].
